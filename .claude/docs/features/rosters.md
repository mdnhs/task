## Access (D4, D5)

`Role.rosterLevel` = `office` or `supervisor`; `rosterAccess()` returns `{scope, locationIds, editDraft, editPublished, approve}`.

| Who | Sees | Draft week | Published week |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| owner, admin, Roster Manager (`office`) | all locations; a Roster Manager loses `except` locations (`access.json` `limits`) | edit | edit with a reason, approve |
| Location Supervisor (`supervisor`) | granted locations only (`grants`) | edit, approve claims and swaps | read, change request (D7: no unpublish) |
| other manager-home roles with `roster: read` | all | read | read |
| staff-home roles | own shifts only (`/rosters/mine`) | none | confirm, claim, swap |

Platform role and tenants without M1A: not-in-plan or restricted page. Location Supervisor is its own person (`p-ismail`).
Location Supervisor has read on `tasks` and write on `roster` only: `staff`, `shifts`, `dashboard` and `whatsapp` are `none` (ruling, final review finding 4), because those legacy modules are not location-aware and would show other sites' gaps, incidents, people and shifts. Nav hides them; `/staff-rosters` and `/shifts` show "Access restricted" (smoke covers both). Restoring a module for this role needs location scoping first.

## Rule checks (D9)

`RuleId`: `double_booking`, `approved_leave`, `unavailable`, `daily_rest`, `weekly_rest`, `avg_48h`, `expired_licence`, `left`.
Severity per rule in `rosters/rules.json`: block for `double_booking` and `approved_leave`, warn for the rest. `avgWeekReferenceWeeks` = 17 (about the Act's 4 months), data not code.
Rest (11h daily, 24h weekly) and hours use real elapsed time, so DST nights are right. `weekly_rest` reports once per person per overlapping window (first shift carries it). `unavailable` is a "not available" window per weekday, overnight windows are split at midnight.

## Publish, lock, change request, history (FR-RS-30..37, 44)

- `publishWeek`: warnings never stop it. A blocking violation needs an office role and a reason. A supervisor can't publish over a block.
- Published weeks lock for supervisors. Office `editPublished` needs a reason and clears the person's confirmation.
- Change patches are whitelisted (`date`, `start`, `end`, `unpaidBreakMin`, `personId`, `templateId`, `role`; the office edit may also carry `weekId` for a move). A request cannot move a shift to another week. When the person, template or day changes, the person must be eligible (always refused otherwise); a change that adds a blocking violation throws `BlockedError` unless an office role gives a reason (the edit's own reason, or "Approve anyway" on a request). Checked in this browser's shifts only (see mock limits).
- Remove a shift: `removeShift`. Draft week: any role that may edit it. Published week: office only, with a reason. History keeps the whole shift as `before`.
- `addShiftsChecked`, `moveShiftChecked` and `removeShift` are the access and lock guards the view calls; `moveShiftChecked` is for draft weeks (published moves go through `editPublished`).
- Supervisor on a published week: `requestChange` (reason) -> office `decideRequest` approve or reject (requests panel).
- Every change writes a `HistoryEntry` (`weekId` null for location-level changes such as supervisor grants), shown in the history panel.

## Repeat and templates (FR-RS-22, 26..29; D10)

- Repeat copies a week to the next one, or N weeks (all or nothing, `repeatManyChecked`). Refuses a published target; a draft target needs an explicit replace; target must be a Monday.
- Each copy is re-checked. Ruling: only block-severity violations turn a copied shift into an open shift plus a conflict line. A warn violation keeps the person and lists a conflict with `open: false`. A public holiday is an information line only (the person stays), shown only when the shift has no violation.
- Week templates (`week-templates.json`) save a week and apply it to a week with no roster. Applied at another location, a person from elsewhere becomes open and the shift takes that site's matching shift type (`templateId: ""` when none).
- `autoRepeatDays` on a week: `autoRepeatDue` finds weeks due for the next copy.
- Shift types (`templates.json`) cannot be deleted while a shift uses them (`canDeleteTemplate`).

## Open shifts and swaps (FR-RS-38, 42, 43; D6)

- A member claims an open shift on a published, upcoming week if eligible (`ineligible`: left, or required licence missing or expired). A swap is a hand-over request to a named colleague.
- `approverFor(week, location, access)`: office roles always (within their locations). A supervisor on a draft week, and on a published week only if `location.swapApprovalDelegated`. Staff and read-only never. Nobody approves their own.
- Approval re-runs the rules (`checkAssignRules`). A block stops it unless an office role gives a reason (`BlockedError`).

## Staff self-service (FR-RS-40, 41; D8)

- Confirm each shift (`confirmedAt`), "Seen" state, `.ics` export of own roster, weekly availability (not-available windows), leave request.
- Leave is approved by a supervisor of the person's `homeLocationId`, else an office role for people homed at a location the role sees or nowhere (`canApproveLeave`; a Roster Manager with an `except` never decides for someone homed at the excepted site). Nobody decides their own leave. Approved leave blocks rostering (`approved_leave`).
- Roster shifts and pattern shifts show together on `/rosters/mine` (`combinedRoster`).

## Overlay into shift reports (D3, FR-RS-25)

`overlayRoster` turns each published roster shift into a one-day pattern (id = roster shift id, name `<type> · <location>`) plus a one-day assignment, and carves the person's pattern assignment out of that week. So shift reports, Today and deadlines work unchanged.

- The M1A overlay is no longer applied to shifts, reports or Today (B6 T6.7, I17): the demo roster does not feed reports until B8.
- `getBaseShiftState()` has no overlay. The Shifts admin page uses it so it lists only real patterns (it therefore does not show roster shifts).
- `s-*` staff have no shift-member row, so no report prompt for them.

## Derived gaps (D12)

`gapsFromOpenShifts` (`features/staff-rosters/logic.ts`) makes every upcoming open shift a gap in Staff & Rosters (`GAP-RS-<shiftId>`), labelled "From roster", linking back to `/rosters?location=&week=`. Cut by roster access (a supervisor sees only their sites). Recording cover there does not assign the roster shift in this mock.

## Privacy

A caller who does not see every location (a supervisor, or an office role with an `except`) never receives another location's shifts, history, requests, templates or leave, nor its people by home: `getRosterData`, `getLocationAdminData`, `getRosterExchange` and `getLeaveQueue` cut the workforce with `cutMembers`/`scopedMembers` (on a visible shift, a visible home for office roles, or a licence a visible shift type requires; licences cut to those types; a hidden home is dropped, and a supervisor gets no home at all). `view.ts` cuts the view by `rosterAccess` on the server. A clash at a site the caller can't see reads "Already rostered elsewhere" with no site or time.
Server actions (`actions.ts`) take the caller from the session and run the rules over all locations:
- `checkWeekRules(weekId, shifts)`: the caller also posts their own shifts of that week (made in this session, so a new week or shift is checked). `sanitizePosted` keeps only shifts at locations the session may see, of people in the workforce, dated inside the week (a new week: one Monday), then they replace the same-id seed shifts and the seed shifts of that week at the caller's locations. The check runs against everything else and returns violations on those shifts only, generic message for hidden causes. An unknown week answers for the posted shifts.
- `checkAssignRules(pairs)`: for claims and swaps; restricted to visible-location shifts and people tied to that location.
- `getRosterRules` is cut per caller (leave and availability of people on a visible shift only; leave is approved-only with kind and decision blanked).
No hour total reaches a caller who does not see every location: the `avg_48h` text reads "is over the 48-hour weekly average" (week check) or "would go over ..." (claims and swaps), because the total includes hidden-site hours. The browser's own check of its visible shifts may still show a number (visible hours only).
Accepted leak, as a ruling: names of other-site staff holding a licence an in-scope shift type requires (name and that licence only, no home site).

## Mock limits (D13)

- Edits live in client stores until reload; a roster published in a session does not reach server-built shift reports. "The API refuses" (AC-RS-05) shows as disabled controls plus pure guards.
- Staff claims and swap requests made in the session are not on the server, so `checkAssignRules` cannot see them.
- `checkWeekRules` reads mock JSON for the other sites; the caller's own edits come in as posted shifts. A shift moved out of the week being published is not seen in its new week by this call.
- Change checks on published shifts (`editPublished`, `decideRequest`) run over this browser's shifts only, not the server's hidden-site shifts.
- Stores shared between pages use one key (`scope.ts`): `rosterScopeKey` for Rosters, Locations and the exchange (claims, swaps are one list on `/rosters` and `/rosters/mine`, topped up by `mergeById`, so an approved claim never reappears as open); `mineScopeKey` for the My roster only stores. The location form seeds `historyStore` with the same history as Rosters (`LocationAdminData.history`). Rosters reads `locationsStore` (`mergeLocations`), so a location added or renamed in the form shows at once.
- `confirmStore` is not synced with the shift store's `confirmedAt` clear in-session.

## Backend notes (B8)

- **Schema:** migrations 0043 (locations, supervisor grants, office limits, shift types, targets, qualifications, `roles.roster_level`) and 0044 (weeks, shifts, history, change requests, exchanges, leave, availability, week templates, two exactly-once ledgers). All FORCE RLS; `roster_history` keeps plain `week_id`/`shift_id` (no FK) so the log outlives a deleted shift. A membership delete clears only `roster_shifts.user_id` and `roster_exchanges.to_user_id` (column-list SET NULL) and erases the person's own requests, availability and leave.
- **Access boundary** (`api/src/modules/rosters/scope.ts`): `loadRosterCtx` adapts the domain `rosterAccess` (role `roster_level`, supervisor grants, office `except` list). A location the caller cannot see is **404**, identical to a missing one (`assertLocation`); lists are cut in SQL by `inScopeSql` (`IN (visible ids)`, `false` for none or the staff "own" scope, never "all"). `weekEditGate` is the publish lock: no edit right 403 `forbidden`, supervisor on a published week 403 `roster_locked`, office on a published week needs a reason (422 `reason_required`).
- **Lock order** of every roster write: `lockRoster` (one writer per tenant, `ROSTER_LOCK_NS`), `lockActor`, row `FOR UPDATE`, writes, history, notify, audit, publish. `loadRosterCtx(..., "write")` refuses a transaction that does not hold the roster lock.
- New tenants get `roles.roster_level` from the role templates (roles.json `rosterLevel`); existing tenants got it from the 0043 backfill.
- **Locations, shift types, targets, grants, limits** (Task 5, `locations.ts`, `routes.ts`, `write.ts`): reads are cut by scope, writes are office level only (a supervisor or read-only role gets 403 whatever the id; an office role with an except list gets 404 for an excepted site). Roster path ids are loose strings: a non-uuid id answers the same 404 body and headers as a missing, hidden or foreign one and never reaches SQL (`assertLocation`, `isUuid`); a shift type reuses the plain `not_found` code so a hidden site shows no difference. Locations and shift types are archived, never deleted (rostered shifts keep their type; `includeArchived=true` lists them). A zone change recomputes DRAFT shifts only (`instantSql`, version bumped). Supervisor PUT writes history, notices (kind `member`), audit with the old and new set and one `location.supervisors_changed`; an unchanged set writes nothing. `PUT /v1/roster/office-limits/{userId}` is for an office role with no limit of its own; the target must be an active office-level member with roster write. Writes use `rosterWrite` (lock, actor, rows, history, notify, audit, publish).
- **Roles carry-in:** `roles.roster_level` is in the role DTO; `cloneRole` copies it and PATCH `/v1/roles/{id}` accepts `rosterLevel` (`office | supervisor | null`). A caller cannot grant, clone or clear a level above their own (403 `role_escalation`).
- **Member lifecycle** (Task 6, migration 0045, `api/src/modules/members/`): `POST /v1/members/{userId}/deactivate|reactivate` (settings write, so owner and admin; an admin cannot act on an owner). Deactivation takes the RBAC, shift and roster advisory locks (in that order), then the actor and the target row; the last active owner is 409 `last_owner` (B1 count guard, rows locked), oneself 422 `self_deactivation`. Effects: `status = deactivated` and `deactivated_at` (the `left` rule's input: `leftOn` = that date in the tenant zone); open pattern assignments end today (later ones are removed); open roster exchanges are cancelled, pending change requests rejected and pending leave cancelled; roster shifts are NOT touched (the `left` rule flags them); one roster history line per location with upcoming shifts; a notice (kind `member`, name via `{{subject}}`) to those locations' other active supervisors; audit `member.deactivated`; event `member.deactivated`. Sessions are deleted after commit when it was the person's last workspace (sessions are global). The tenant gate already answers 404 to a non-active member and API keys check membership status. Reactivation needs a free seat (409 `seat_limit`), audit `member.reactivated`.
- **Last active:** `requireTenant` reads `last_active_at` with the membership it already loads and, only when it is null or 5+ minutes old, stamps it in the background (one conditional UPDATE, errors swallowed and counted in `last_active_stamp_total{result}`, deps.clock only). `GET /v1/people` returns `status` and `lastActiveAt`; `?status=deactivated|all` needs settings read (Settings -> Users).
- **Qualifications and home location** (`qualifications.ts`, `people.ts`): reads reach the person, an office role (a limited one: homed at or rostered at a visible site, or homed nowhere) and supervisors of a site the person is homed at or rostered at within 90 days; everyone else, a ghost and another tenant's person get the same 404 `member_not_found`. Writes are office only. `expired` is computed on the clock (valid through the expiry date). A PUT replaces the set but keeps the row (and its B11 proof-file link) of a type that stays.

Deactivation sets a 5 s `lock_timeout` before its first advisory lock (55P03 -> 409 `retry`). Qualification keys (shift type `requires` and `member_qualifications.type`) are canonical lower case, so 'First Aid' = 'first aid'; there is no display label column (nothing migrated, a label would be a later migration).

NOT VALID` then `VALIDATE CONSTRAINT` (0045 did not: `memberships` is small and the column is new and null).
- **Leave and availability** (Task 7, migration 0046 adds `leave_requests.decision_note`, `api/src/modules/rosters/leave.ts`): `POST /v1/leave` is the caller's own request (needs roster read only: a `rosterWrite(..., "self")` takes the same lock order as a write but asks `roster:read`), 409 `leave_overlap` against the person's own open or approved leave, 422 `invalid_leave`. Approval (D8): the supervisor of the person's HOME location or an office role whose scope covers it (homed nowhere: any office role; an `except` list never decides for the excepted site); never the requester (403 `self_decision`); a person the caller may not decide for is the same 404 `leave_not_found` as a missing or non-uuid id. `decide` twice is 409 `already_decided`. The approvers told about a request are the home site's supervisors, else the office roles covering it; the requester is named only through the `{{subject}}` token. Events `roster.leave_requested` and `roster.leave_decided` carry ids only; audit `roster.leave_decided` (and `roster.leave_cancelled` for an approved leave). `GET|PUT /v1/roster/availability` is the caller's own "not available" windows; an overnight window is stored as two rows split at midnight (`to` 00:00 means 24:00), the set is replaced atomically.
- **Rule engine on the server** (Task 8, `api/src/modules/rosters/rules.ts`): the rules are the pure `checkShifts` of `packages/domain/src/roster-rules.ts` (R8-1 DST rule through `wallToEpoch`, one zone per location through `RuleContext.zones`); the server only loads the context and applies settings. `loadRuleContext` runs at most 6 queries (settings, shifts with type and site zone, members with qualifications, approved leave, availability, public holidays), reads ALL locations (a hidden site's shifts of the same person still produce double-booking, rest and weekly-hours findings) and widens the window by the averaging weeks and an 8-day margin itself. `visibleLocationIds` is null for a caller who sees every site; otherwise the domain masks: a hidden clash is "Already rostered elsewhere" (no site, time or shift id), a hidden neighbour a generic rest line, and the avg_48h, approved_leave and unavailable texts carry no hour total, dates or windows. Severities live in the tenant setting `roster.rules` (`block | warn | ignore` per rule, default block for double_booking and approved_leave, warn for the rest; `avgWeekReferenceWeeks` 1..52, default 17); `GET /v1/roster/rules` is open to roster read, `PUT` to admin or owner (roster write AND settings write), audit `roster.rules_changed`. `POST /v1/roster/weeks/{id}/check` is a dry run for editors (roster write): `{ weekId, violations, blocking }`, 404 `not_found` for a week of an unseen site, a missing one or a non-uuid id, nothing written. `checkWeek(tx, ctx, weekId, { dropShiftIds, add })` is the entry point later tasks (shift edits, publish, repeat) call; `loadRuleContext` also returns the public holidays of the window for repeat's information line.
- **Round 1 (Tasks 7 and 8 review, AD1 to AD5):** (AD1) `rosterWrite(..., "self")` asks `roster:read` of a person but `roster:write` of an API-key actor (a key acts for its creator): a read-only key is 403 on leave create/cancel and availability PUT. (AD2) `GET /v1/leave` is newest first (created, then id) with a keyset cursor (`limit` 1..200 default 50, `nextCursor`, garbage cursor 400 `invalid_cursor`); cancelling an APPROVED leave tells the deciders, publishes `roster.leave_cancelled` and audits, a pending cancel is silent; a leave whose first day is today or earlier (the person's site zone, else the tenant zone, on `deps.clock`) cannot be cancelled (422 `leave_started`). D8 deciders are "home supervisor OR office role in scope"; the notification audience is BOTH groups when both exist; nobody is told of their own request. (AD3) for a caller who cannot see a site, every violation derived from a hidden shift (overlap, daily rest, weekly rest, 48 h average) is ONE generic violation per shift: rule `elsewhere`, "Already rostered elsewhere", the worst severity of the collapsed ones, no ids, hours, dates or counts. (AD4) `GET /v1/roster/rules` carries a strong ETag; `PUT` needs `If-Match` (428 missing, 412 stale with the current ETag), is PATCH-like (a rule or the window left out keeps its stored value) and answers the new ETag. (AD5) `ignore` is only for the non-statutory rules; `daily_rest`, `weekly_rest` and `avg_48h` take `warn | block` (422 `invalid_rules`), and a stored `ignore` on them is read as `warn`.

- **Draft weeks and shifts** (Task 9, `api/src/modules/rosters/{weeks,shifts}.ts`): `POST /v1/roster/weeks` creates an empty draft for a real Monday (422 `invalid_week_start`; a repeat is 409 `week_exists` with `weekId`; an archived site 409). `GET /v1/roster/weeks?locationId=&from=&to=&view=` and `GET /v1/roster/weeks/{id}` return one payload: weeks with seven `days` (domain `dayColumns`: count of named people against the summed target, short, over; an open shift never counts), `shifts` (wall strings plus stored `startAt`/`endAt`), the site's `shiftTypes` with targets and the `members` the caller may pick from (active people homed at a visible site, everyone on a shown shift, those homed nowhere only for a role that sees every site; a supervisor gets no home location). Views day, week, fortnight, month (`rosterRange`, a week that overlaps the range is included, at most 62 days); staff (scope own) get an empty list. `POST /v1/roster/weeks/{id}/shifts` assigns several people at once (`userIds` with `null` = open shift; start, end, break and role default from the type and can differ per entry; end at or before start is overnight and the shift stays on its start date; instants come from `wallToEpoch` in the site zone, so a start in the spring gap is the transition instant and one in the autumn fold the first occurrence). It is all-or-nothing. `PATCH /v1/roster/shifts/{id}` (`{version, patch, reason?}`; keys date, start, end, unpaidBreakMin, userId, shiftTypeId, role; another week is 422 `cannot_move_week`, another key 422 `invalid_patch`; a retype copies the new type's times unless the patch gives them; no change writes nothing). `DELETE /v1/roster/shifts/{id}[?version=&reason=]` removes the row and history keeps it whole as `before`. **Eligibility** is always 422 `ineligible` with `errors[{userId, date, reason}]` (`left` for a deactivated or unknown member, `qualification_missing`, `qualification_expired` judged on the shift date) whatever the tenant's rule severities. **Rule gate**: the week is checked before and after the change (`checkWeek` runs INSIDE `rosterWrite`, under the roster lock) and only what the change caused counts: a new block is 422 `rule_blocked` (with `violations`) unless an office role gives a `reason` (the override is in history `after.overrides`), a new warning comes back in `violations` and never stops a draft edit. An identical assigned shift (same person, date, type, times) is 409 `shift_exists` (double click, retry); open shifts may repeat. Every shift write bumps `roster_weeks.version` (publish sends the version it saw) and `roster_shifts.version`. History actions `week_created`, `shift_added`, `shift_moved`, `shift_removed` (ids only) and audit `roster.week_created|shift_added|shift_moved|shift_removed`; no domain event for a draft. A published week is 403 `roster_locked` for a supervisor and 422 `reason_required` for office without a reason (`weekEditGate`, called by the shift helper, not by routes); the office edit is Task 10 (below).
